> Here you can freely contribute personal feedback on your experiences participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program, helping the FCAJ team improve areas for development based on the following categories:

### Overall Assessment

**1. Work Environment**  
- The work environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always ready to support whenever I encounter difficulties, even outside working hours. The workspace is neat and comfortable, helping me focus better.
- Every week there is a Saturday event that allows me to network, learn a lot of new knowledge, and get acquainted and collaborate with other FCAJ members.

**2. Support from Mentors / Admin Team**  
- The admin team members are enthusiastic, helping answer my questions during learning and working on the workshop project.
- Fast and easy-to-understand support.

**3. Relevance Between Work and Academic Major**  
- My academic major is Computer Science, and learning about AWS services helped me understand more about deploying and applying research projects into real-world business scenarios. This further reinforced my knowledge. Therefore, the work aligns very well with my major.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**  
- During the internship, I gained additional knowledge from the senior members and team peers.
- Learned more knowledge about AWS and project deployment.
- Expanded my understanding of future career paths, especially AI Engineer, MLOps, and DevOps.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects one another, works seriously yet maintains a cheerful atmosphere. When there are urgent projects, everyone pulls together and supports each other regardless of position. This made me feel like a valued part of the team, even as an intern.

**6. Policies & Benefits for Interns**  
- The company arranges office schedules flexibly when necessary. Additionally, being able to participate in internal events is a major plus.
- The company supports providing official internship stamps for students.

### Additional Questions
- What are you **most satisfied with** during your internship?  
- Getting to meet many peers in the industry, networking, learning a lot of new knowledge and AWS expertise, which greatly benefits my career orientation.  
- If recommending to friends, would you **advise them to intern here**? Why?  
- Yes, because interning here provides great conditions and enables strong connections with FCAJ members.
